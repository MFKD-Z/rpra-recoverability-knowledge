# B2 Risks and Next Step

## Remaining interpretation risks

- The evidence is case-specific to one scalar three-pass configuration and five fixed grids.
- The experiment is a deterministic finite numerical challenge, not an inferential or physical-validation study.
- Exact Decimal upward mapping is used here. The historical tolerance-adjusted helper is known to differ for synthetic minimally-above-grid values, although the prior frozen 400-state cases were unaffected.
- Coarse-grid agreement is 112/161 because 49 conservative false rejections occur near the boundary; reporting only zero false acceptances would conceal this resolution cost.
